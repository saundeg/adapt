Where are you with AI at work?

A ten-minute self-assessment for professional services colleagues, based on the five domains of the ADAPT framework.

You'll read 30 short statements, six for each domain, and say how much each one sounds like you. At the end you'll see where you sit in each domain, your strongest area, a suggested focus for development, and practical next steps.

AwareYou understand the basics and have started to experiment.
PractisingYou use AI purposefully and thoughtfully in your everyday work.
LeadingYou shape how your team and service use AI, and support others.

This is a tool for reflection and development conversations, not a performance measure. Answer honestly: there are no right answers. Your responses stay in this browser and are not sent anywhere.
